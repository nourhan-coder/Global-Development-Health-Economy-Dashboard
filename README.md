# 🌍 Global Development: Health, Economy & Trade Analytics

## 📌 Executive Summary
A comprehensive global overview investigating trends across 200+ countries regarding economic output (GDP), inflation, healthcare outcomes, and international trade dynamics.

## 🔑 Key Insights & Analytics
- **Wealth vs. Longevity Gap:** Strong positive correlation between GDP per capita and life expectancy. High-income nations (e.g., Andorra, Singapore) exceed **81–84 years**, whereas Africa averages **52.38 years**.
- **Infant Mortality Concentration:** High infant mortality remains overwhelmingly concentrated in low-income economies (Angola: 191/1000, Afghanistan: 163/1000).
- **Economic Disparity:** Asia represents **60.67%** (~4 Billion) of global population, yet wealth remains heavily concentrated in major global powers.
- **Hyperinflation Risk:** Zimbabwe (**133%**) and Dominican Republic (**55%**) record the highest inflation bottlenecks severely impacting purchasing power.
